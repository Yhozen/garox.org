Hello, my friend.

I don't know who you are. Maybe you opened this from the Posts folder and it's sitting in a
little window. Maybe someone sent you the link and it took over the whole screen. Either way,
you're inside the thing I'm about to tell you about.

Now, seriously: I've been building this page for years, or at least planning to. The
[first commit](https://github.com/Yhozen/garox.org/commit/5ac50dc) is from 2015, and in 2022 I
wrote one called [add desktop functionality](https://github.com/Yhozen/garox.org/commit/8dc3c09).
An old Mac, in the browser. In 2024 I got close, close enough to see I didn't like it. I didn't
have the tools or the time to make it the way I wanted.

When PostHog shipped their landing page, a whole desktop in the browser, I almost deleted the
repo. It was good. Really good. And I told myself the window had closed. If I finished mine now
it would just look late.

So I stopped. For a long time the only commit was me
[updating my About](https://github.com/Yhozen/garox.org/commit/0938951). Look at the commit
message. Even that one's a sad face.

But it was never about the desktop.

I want to share things. What I'm building, what I'm learning, what I'm
[listening to](/now-playing), what I actually think. And everywhere I could share it, someone
else decides who sees it. A feed ranks it. An algorithm scores it. You start writing for the
algorithm and the algorithm starts writing you.

Not here. There's no feed on this page. No likes, no reach, no algorithm deciding what you get
to read. Just what I think, or what I want to show you, in the order I choose.

What finally gave me the tools was AI. It got good enough that I could take the idea I'd been
carrying since 2015 and make it concrete. To be honest, I don't know if it's there yet, but at
least it's close enough that I feel comfortable sharing it with you.

Somewhere along the way I stopped caring what people would think. Including whether this looks
late. What matters is what I really want to put out into the world. This is where I do it.

I'll be here, friend.
