# On dithering

Placeholder. The covers in [Now Playing](/?w=now-playing) are drawn with one bit per
pixel using Atkinson dithering, the method Bill Atkinson wrote for the original
Macintosh.

It spreads only three quarters of each pixel's error to its neighbors, so highlights
blow out to white and shadows stay crisp. Read more on
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atkinson_dithering).
